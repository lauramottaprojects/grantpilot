How it works

Every match runs through our five-agent funding team — transparent, evidence-based, and verified before it reaches you.

  1. ResearchWe scan the live funding database against your profile.
  2. DesignWe score every match on sector, size, location and need.
  3. BuildWe assemble your match report, with evidence for every pick.
  4. CommunicateWe explain the top pick first — and why it fits.
  5. VerifyA quality pass confirms every name, amount and deadline.
